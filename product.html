Is this oil really cold-pressed?

Yes. Seeds are pressed slowly in a wooden ghani without heat, so the oil keeps its natural pungency and nutrients.

Why is it so pungent (ঝাঁঝ)?

That sharp aroma comes from natural compounds in mustard seed. Refined oils lose it — ours keeps it, which is how you know it is pure.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Absolutely. Cash on delivery is available all over Bangladesh. You can also pay with bKash or Nagad.

How should I store it?

Keep the bottle tightly closed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. No refrigeration needed.
